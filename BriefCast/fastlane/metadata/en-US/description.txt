Audexa is your personal AI radio that transforms the latest news, technology, business, and more into engaging podcast-style audio briefings tailored just for you.

KEY FEATURES:

Daily Briefings
Connect your Gmail to receive a personalized morning briefing that summarizes your important emails, calendar events, and top news. Start every day informed and ready.

AI-Generated Podcasts
Choose from dozens of topics including Technology, Finance, Sports, Science, Entertainment, and more. Audexa generates fresh, high-quality podcast episodes daily using advanced AI voices.

Dual-Host Conversations
Enjoy natural-sounding discussions between two AI hosts. Choose from multiple voice pairs powered by advanced AI to find the perfect sound for your listening experience.

Personalized For You
Select your interests and Audexa curates content specifically for you. Bookmark your favorite topics, hide what you don't want, and discover new shows in the Discover tab.

Multi-Language Support
Listen to podcasts in your preferred language. Audexa supports English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese, Italian, and Hindi.

Sleep Timer & Playback Controls
Set a sleep timer, skip forward or back, and enjoy seamless playback with background audio support and lock screen controls.

Ad-Free Option
Upgrade to enjoy uninterrupted listening without audio ad breaks. Support indie development while getting the cleanest listening experience.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Sign in with Google
2. Choose your topics of interest
3. Optionally link your email for personalized briefings
4. Listen to AI-generated episodes on demand

Your privacy matters. We only read email subjects and senders to create briefing summaries. We never store your email content or share your data with third parties.
